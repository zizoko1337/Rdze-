# Tekstury plazmowej dubeltówki

Wygenerowane wbudowanym narzędziem `image_gen`, przy użyciu umiejętności `imagegen`. Gotowe pliki skopiowano do projektu; gra ładuje lokalne zasoby. Model i animacja są geometrią 3D w Direct3D 11.

## Drewno

[public/assets/shotgun-walnut.png](public/assets/shotgun-walnut.png)

Pełny prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Production game texture asset, seamless tileable square albedo of exquisite premium oiled dark walnut hardwood for a luxury handcrafted sci-fi plasma double-barrel shotgun. Straight orthographic material close-up filling whole square canvas. Rich chocolate brown with warm reddish golden grain, longitudinal flowing fine wood fibers and subtle figured waves, polished satin oil finish, sophisticated restrained luxury woodworking. Even diffuse lighting with no directional cast light or baked reflections. Fine sharp material grain that remains readable when wrapped on small 3D stock and fore-end. No firearm, objects, edges, logos, text, labels, vignette, background, presentation sphere, or UI. Opaque flat material texture only.
