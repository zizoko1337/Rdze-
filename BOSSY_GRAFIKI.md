# Prompty sprite'ów bossów

Tryb: wbudowane `imagegen`, `transparent_background: true`. Pliki finalne: `public/assets/`. Oryginały zachowano w katalogu wygenerowanych obrazów Codex.

Wspólny prefiks pierwszych czterech promptów:

> Game asset: a single full-body alien boss cutout for a native 2.5D sci-fi survival game. Detailed painted dark chitin, grotesque alien creature, strong readable silhouette, glossy organic materials, 3/4 front view seen slightly from above, matching high quality pre-rendered RTS monster sprites. Entire creature visible centered with transparent padding on every side; genuine transparent background. No scene, no ground plane, no text, no UI, no cast ground shadow.
