# Kapitan — dwie klatki portretu

Pliki projektu: `public/assets/captain-closed.png` i `public/assets/captain-open.png`. Oba powstały wbudowanym narzędziem `image_gen` przy użyciu umiejętności `imagegen`, bez CLI i klucza API. Pierwszy portret wygenerowano od podstaw; drugi utworzono przez edycję pierwszego. Obrazy są nieprzezroczyste, kwadratowe i zachowują to samo kadrowanie. Gra ładuje lokalne PNG; oryginały pozostają w katalogu generatora.

Portret zamkniętych ust — pełny prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production portrait for an in-game science-fiction radio communications HUD. Subject: a human male starship captain, late forties, weathered calm face, short dark hair with silver at the temples, neat short beard, subtle eyebrow scar, wearing a charcoal navy armored officer uniform with restrained cyan collar lights and a compact headset microphone. Front-facing head and shoulders, looking directly at camera, mouth fully CLOSED in a neutral attentive expression, lips together. Semi-realistic painted 3D game portrait, grounded industrial cyberpunk tone matching a dark alien-planet defense game, readable at a tiny HUD size. Composition: square crop, whole head with clear margin above the hair, both shoulders visible, face centered at exactly the vertical centre, symmetrical camera at eye height. Dim softly blurred navy-blue bridge background with very restrained cyan lights, consistent soft cool key light and gentle warm rim on the face. No text, rank labels, lettering, UI frame, border, speech bubbles, hologram interference or additional people. Important: this image will be the closed-mouth frame of a two-frame talking portrait; use a still, stable pose and an unobstructed mouth that can be edited to open without changing anything else.

Portret otwartych ust — edycja `captain-closed.png`, pełny prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached closed-mouth captain portrait. Create the second frame of a two-frame talking animation. Change ONLY the lips and immediately surrounding mouth area: gently open the captain's mouth as if speaking a short syllable, with a small natural dark mouth opening and a restrained glimpse of upper teeth. Preserve exactly the same identity, face proportions, hair, beard, eyes, gaze, eyebrow scar, expression, headset microphone, uniform, lighting, background, colors, crop and pixel alignment. The head, shoulders, camera and mouth centre must not move. No larger smile, no exaggerated shouting, no extra objects, no text. Output a square image at the same resolution and framing as the input, opaque background preserved.
