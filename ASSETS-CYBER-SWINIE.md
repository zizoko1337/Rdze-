# Cyberświnia z profilu

Plik projektu: `public/assets/cyber-pig-profile.png`. Wygenerowano wbudowanym narzędziem `image_gen` przy użyciu umiejętności `imagegen`, z przezroczystym tłem. Wybrany PNG skopiowano do repozytorium. Oryginał pozostał w katalogu generatora. Sprawdzono kanał alfa: narożniki i górny margines mają alfa 0.

Świnia jest pokazana z profilu, z pyskiem i laserami skierowanymi w prawo. Renderer odbija grafikę poziomo zgodnie z kierunkiem biegu rzutowanym na prawo/lewo aktualnej kamery, także w widoku taktycznym. Klatka, drony, liny, lasery i kurz powstają w silniku gry.

Pełny prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent 2D side-profile unit sprite for an existing dark science-fiction alien-planet tower-defense game. Primary request: one powerful cybernetic pig sprinting, unmistakable pig anatomy, full body in STRICT SIDE PROFILE, head and snout pointing RIGHT, curly tail on LEFT, all four short muscular legs visible in a dynamic running gait, underside and feet fully in frame. Heavy low broad boar-pig silhouette with natural muted pink-grey skin, chunky worn gunmetal armor over shoulders and back, exposed mechanical leg joints, tiny cyan power conduits and cyan cybernetic eye, compact twin laser emitters attached to a back-mounted turret pointing forward RIGHT. Tough industrial cyberpunk machinery, readable at small in-game size, semi-realistic painterly 3D game-sprite finish, restrained color accents and crisp silhouette. Orthographic side view at the pig's shoulder height, no front view and no three-quarter view. Composition: single isolated pig centered, generous transparent margin on all sides especially above the turret and around the feet, wide landscape canvas approximately 3:2. Neutral diffuse lighting, balanced material contrast, no directional cast shadow. Actual transparent background. No cage, drones, floor, scenery, dust, smoke, laser beams, glow trails, text, logos, border, additional characters or sprite sheet. The whole unit including tail, snout, ears, gun and feet must remain inside frame.
